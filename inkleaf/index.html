InkLeaf

An iPhone app for keeping the passages and quotes you want to remember.

Save passages and quotes

InkLeaf keeps each passage or quote with its author and source. Favorite the ones you want to find again, group them into collections, and search the whole library.

Find them again

Browse by author, source or tag, or flip through a collection to rediscover what you saved.

  • Favorite what you want to keep close
  • Group quotes into collections
  • Search your whole library
  • Flip through your collections
  • Sync with iCloud
